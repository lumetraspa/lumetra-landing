Lumetra. Supplier Information
For Suppliers & Distributors

You received an order from a Lumetra client.

Lumetra is an inventory management platform built for medical spas. When a medspa's stock drops below a set threshold, Lumetra automatically sends a reorder request on behalf of the practice owner. The email you received is a legitimate purchase order — configured and approved by your client.

How it works

01

The owner configured the order

Your client set their own reorder thresholds and quantities inside Lumetra. Nothing is sent without their prior setup and approval.

02

The email contains all order details

Each reorder email includes the exact product name, quantity requested, current stock level, medspa name, and owner contact info. Process it as you would any manual PO.

03

Replies go directly to the owner

If you need to confirm or follow up, simply reply. Your response goes straight to the medspa owner — not to Lumetra.

04

Questions about the order?

Contact the medspa owner using the info in the original email. They handle all order decisions, quantities, and delivery details.
