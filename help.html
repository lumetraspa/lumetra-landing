Managing inventory

Inventory

Go to Inventory → + Add Product. You can search our built-in catalog of 90+ medspa products (Botox, Juvederm, skincare, supplies) or type a custom name. Fill in current stock, your minimum threshold, and reorder quantity, then save.
Use the Import CSV button in the Inventory tab. Download the template, fill it in with your products, and upload it. You'll see a preview before anything is saved. The template columns are: Name, Category, Unit, Current Stock, Min Threshold, Reorder Qty.
Click the edit icon on any product row and update the Current Stock field. Stock levels don't sync automatically from your practice management software yet — manual updates keep things accurate.
  • OK — stock is above your minimum threshold. No action needed.
  • Low / Active — stock has dropped below threshold. A reorder has been or will be triggered.
  • BOOST — Boost Mode is active. The threshold is temporarily doubled to handle a demand spike. Reverts automatically after the boost period ends.
Thresholds & reorders

Thresholds & reorders

Your minimum threshold is the stock level that triggers a reorder. When your current stock drops to or below this number, Lumetra sends you an approval email. A good rule of thumb is 25% of your monthly usage — so if you use 20 vials/month, set a threshold of 5.
When you edit a product and enter your Avg Monthly Usage, Lumetra suggests a threshold (25% of monthly usage) and a reorder quantity (50% of monthly usage). These are starting points — adjust them based on your supplier's lead time.
Boost Mode temporarily doubles a product's reorder threshold for a set period. Use it before a high-demand event like a holiday promotion or a new treatment launch. The product shows a yellow BOOST badge, and it reverts automatically when the boost period ends.

The bot

Suppliers

Lumetra sends a professional purchase order email directly to your supplier's order email address. The email is signed with your name and practice name, includes the product and quantity, and asks the supplier to reply to confirm. You are CC'd on every order.
The purchase order emails are written as professional POs from you, not from Lumetra. The reply-to is your email address, so any supplier responses go directly to you. Suppliers see a normal, professional order — not an automated system message.
Yes. Add as many suppliers as you need in the Suppliers tab. Each product is linked to a specific supplier, so reorders go to the right place automatically.
